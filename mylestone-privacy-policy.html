Privacy Policy

Mylestone • Effective Date: September 11, 2026

🔒 TL;DR: We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Your timeline entries stay 100% on your device.

1. Overview

We respect your personal privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Mylestone mobile application (package com.masoftware.timelineapp).

2. Data Collection & Usage

We believe in absolute data privacy. Here is what that means in practice:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or account details. The app has no sign-up and no user accounts.
  • No Tracking: We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • No Permissions: The app requests no Android permissions at all — no internet, camera, contacts, storage, or location access.
  • Local Storage: Everything you enter — entry titles, notes, dates and times, your custom entry types, and checklist items — is saved strictly in a private database on your own device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Deletion: Deleting an entry removes it from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or AdMob). None of your interaction data is transmitted outside your smartphone.

4. Adding an Entry to Your Calendar

Mylestone offers an optional "add to calendar" action. When you tap it, the app opens your own calendar app's new-event screen with the entry's title, note, date, and time already filled in. Nothing is saved unless you confirm it there, and the information goes only to the calendar app you chose — never to us. The app does not read your calendar and does not request calendar permission.

5. Device Backup

Android's built-in backup feature is left enabled for this app. If you have device backup switched on in your Android settings, your operating system may include Mylestone's data in the backup it stores in your own Google account, so your entries can be restored when you set up a new phone. That backup is handled entirely by Android and Google under their terms — we never see it. You can turn it off at any time in your device's Settings › Google › Backup.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our application is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years old.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published directly on this page with a revised date.
