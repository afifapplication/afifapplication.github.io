Solat Insight Privacy Policy

Developed by MASoftware  •  Effective Date: August 27, 2026
TL;DR

🔒 Your prayer records, settings, and history stay on your device. We use your location only to show accurate prayer times, and we never sell or share your data with advertisers.

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Solat Insight ("the app") accesses, how it is used, and what stays on your device. Solat Insight is developed and maintained by MASoftware.

2 Location Data

The app may request access to your device's location to:

  • Determine your nearest official JAKIM prayer zone (for users in Malaysia), or
  • Calculate prayer times directly from your coordinates (for users outside Malaysia)

Your location is processed only on your device to work out prayer times. It is never stored on our servers, logged, or shared with any third party. Location access is entirely optional — you can deny it and select your prayer zone manually instead at any time from within the app.

3 Prayer Time Data

To show accurate, up-to-date prayer schedules, the app sends your selected zone code to a prayer time provider (JAKIM e-Solat data, accessed via api.waktusolat.app). Only the zone code is sent — no personal information, device identifiers, or precise location is included in this request.

4 Data Stored On Your Device

The following information is saved locally on your device only, in a private on-device database:

DataPurpose
Prayer tracking recordsMarked as done, missed, or excused
Qada (missed prayer) recordsTrack and log make-up prayers
Selected prayer zone / location modeShow your correct prayer times
Language & theme preferencePersonalize the app's appearance
Cached prayer schedulesAllow the app to work offline

This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It stays on your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

5 Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following external services to function:

  • JAKIM e-Solat / api.waktusolat.app — provides official Malaysian prayer time data
  • Device location & geocoding services — provided by your phone's operating system, used to detect your location and, where relevant, a readable place name

6 What We Don't Do

  • No accounts We do not require sign-up, and we do not collect your name, email, or phone number
  • No ads We do not show advertisements or use ad networks
  • No analytics/tracking We do not use analytics SDKs, behavioral tracking, or device fingerprinting
  • No data sale We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party

7 Children's Privacy

Solat Insight is suitable for general audiences and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal identifiers from any user, no such data is knowingly collected from children either.

8 Your Choices

  • You may deny or revoke location permission at any time via your device settings
  • You may select your prayer zone manually instead of using automatic location
  • You may delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing its app data

9 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
